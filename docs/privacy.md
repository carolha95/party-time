# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 24, 2026

Party Time is a personal event invitation app. This policy explains how we handle your information.

## What we collect

- **Name and phone number** — provided by the event host when adding you as a guest
- **RSVP response** — your attendance status, guest count, and any optional note you leave

## How we use it

- To send you an SMS invitation with a link to RSVP
- To send optional event reminders
- To show the event host who is attending

## What we don't do

- We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties
- We do not use your information for marketing or advertising
- We do not store payment or financial information

## Data retention

Your information is kept only for the duration of the event. The event host may delete guest data at any time.

## Opting out

- Reply **STOP** to any text message to stop receiving messages
- Contact the event host directly to have your information removed

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the event host who invited you.
